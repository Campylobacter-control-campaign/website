Science

Where does Campylobacter exposure come from, which transmission routes matter most, and where can prevention have the greatest impact?

Our approach

From sampling to prevention

CCC connects One Health field studies with genomics, epidemiology and modelling to turn surveillance into practical decisions.

1

Sample

Study people, animals, water and environments together.

2

Sequence

Characterise isolates, metagenomes, pathogen diversity and AMR.

3

Attribute

Use genomic and machine-learning approaches to identify likely sources.

4

Model

Combine evidence and local knowledge to compare pathways and interventions.

5

Prevent

Prioritise feasible upstream interventions, including water, livestock and vaccine-informed strategies.
