From pathogen genomes to practical prevention.

CCC brings together field studies, genomics, epidemiology and modelling to understand where Campylobacter transmission comes from — and how to reduce it.

Explore the science Meet the consortium

What we do

Follow transmission. Find leverage points.

Sample

Study people, animals and environments together to identify routes of exposure.

Sequence

Use whole-genome and metagenomic data to connect strains, reservoirs and disease.

Prevent

Combine source attribution and modelling to target practical interventions upstream.
